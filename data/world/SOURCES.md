# The world's map

`world.tsw` (land, lakes, rivers and the borders between countries) and `places.json` (the world's towns and cities)
are made from [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com) (1:10m and 1:50m), which is in the public domain, by
`tools/world_bake.py`. The Route planner TV shows them (`scripts/world_map.gd`, `scripts/world_view.gd`).
